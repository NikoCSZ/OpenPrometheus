# Safety Information

## Disclaimer

OpenPrometheus is an experimental research and development project. All designs, documentation, and software are provided without any guarantee of safety, reliability, regulatory compliance, or fitness for a particular purpose.

The project is currently under development and has not been validated or certified for operational use.

## Potential Hazards

Laser Powder Bed Fusion (LPBF) involves significant hazards, including but not limited to:

- **Laser radiation:** High-power, potentially invisible laser radiation can cause permanent eye injuries, skin injuries, and fires.
- **Metal powders:** Fine metal powders may present inhalation, combustion, explosion, and contamination hazards.
- **Inert gases:** Argon and nitrogen can displace oxygen and create potentially fatal oxygen-deficient atmospheres.
- **Thermal hazards:** Heated components, molten metal, and hot particles may cause burns or ignite surrounding materials.
- **Electrical and mechanical hazards:** High electrical currents, moving mechanisms, and unexpected machine operation can cause serious injuries.

## Safety Requirements

Any operational implementation requires a dedicated safety assessment and appropriate protective measures.

These may include certified or appropriately validated laser containment, independent safety interlocks, suitable powder-handling and filtration equipment, atmosphere monitoring, electrical protection, fire protection, and emergency shutdown systems.

Ordinary 3D-printed enclosures, standard motion-control firmware, and unverified protective equipment must not be assumed to provide adequate protection.

## Development Status

OpenPrometheus is not a validated, ready-to-operate metal 3D printer.

Published designs and specifications may be incomplete, untested, or subject to modification. Their availability does not constitute an assurance that they can be assembled or operated safely.

Testing involving high-power lasers or hazardous metal powders should only be conducted in appropriately equipped facilities under qualified supervision.
